# Larpy (polish): do sprawdzenia przez natywnego speakera

Sprawdź naturalność, formę grzecznościową, pisownię i czy wyjaśnienia są prawdziwe. Wszystko napisane z głowy przez AI. Poprawki wpisz w tabelę albo komentarzem.

## At the café (kawiarnia)

**Scena:** You walk into a café in Kraków. You are hungry and thirsty, and the waiter is waiting with his notepad.  
**Cel:** Order something to drink and something to eat, then pay.  
**Rozmówca:** Kelner  
**Zakończenia:** win: The waiter is impressed by how well you speak Polish. „To na koszt firmy!” – the meal is on the house. · partial: Your order reached the table, though you had to correct yourself several times. The waiter smiles and brings the bill. · fail: The waiter has had enough: he did not understand you several times in a row, took it as an insult and asks you to leave.  

### Poziom 1: Coffee, a roll and cake  (typed=0, hint=none, cierpliwość 6)

Nowe słowa: się = oneself (reflexive: zastanawiam się = I am thinking), zastanawiam = I am thinking, I am wondering (zastanawiać się), oto = here is, here are, picia = drinking, a drink (genitive of picie: do picia = to drink), mlekiem = with milk (instrumental of mleko), cukrem = with sugar (instrumental of cukier), cukru = sugar (genitive of cukier), a = and, but (contrasting), jedzenia = eating, food (genitive of jedzenie: do jedzenia = to eat), bułkę = roll (accusative of bułka), masłem = with butter (instrumental of masło), serem = with cheese (instrumental of ser), ciasta = of cake (genitive of ciasto), smacznego = enjoy your meal (literally: of tasty)

| # | Rozmówca | Uczeń (poprawnie) | Wyjaśnienie |
|---|---|---|---|

*Scena:* A rainy morning in Kraków. You came to Poland to meet Marek, your pen friend, but he has not arrived yet. You sit down in a café and wait.  
*Cel:* Order a coffee, a roll and a piece of cake, and find out what happened to Marek.  
*Zakończenia:* win: The waiter puts an envelope on the table: „Pan Marek zostawił to dla pana”. Inside is a note: „Spóźnię się. Zjedzmy jutro rano śniadanie razem. M.” Now you have a plan for tomorrow. · partial: Your order arrived, and the waiter hands you Marek's envelope, although he barely understood what you wanted. Marek apologises and invites you for tomorrow. · fail: The waiter does not understand you and does not know who you are. Marek's envelope goes to someone else, and you walk out into the rain with an empty stomach.

| 1 | Dzień dobry! Co podać? *(Hello! What can I get you?)* | **Dzień dobry! Jeszcze się zastanawiam.** *(Greet the waiter and say you are still deciding.)* | „Zastanawiam się” means “I am thinking it over”; „jeszcze” means “still / yet”. |
| 2 | Oto menu. Co podać do picia? *(Here is the menu. What can I get you to drink?)* | **Poproszę kawę.** *(Order a coffee (politely).)* | „Poproszę” means “I would like, please”; coffee takes the accusative: kawa → kawę. |
| 3 | Z mlekiem? *(With milk?)* | **Tak, z mlekiem, poproszę.** *(Say yes to milk.)* | After „z” (with) comes the instrumental: mleko → mlekiem. |
| 4 | Z cukrem? *(With sugar?)* | **Nie, bez cukru, dziękuję.** *(Decline sugar.)* | „Bez” (without) is followed by the genitive: cukier → cukru. |
| 5 | Czy podać też wodę? *(Shall I also bring some water?)* | **Tak, poproszę wodę.** *(Say yes to water.)* | Accusative of a feminine noun: woda → wodę. |
| 6 | A co podać do jedzenia? *(And what can I get you to eat?)* | **Poproszę bułkę.** *(Order a roll.)* | Accusative of a feminine noun: bułka → bułkę. |
| 7 | Z masłem czy z serem? *(With butter or with cheese?)* | **Z serem, poproszę.** *(Choose cheese for the roll.)* | After „z” the instrumental: ser → serem. |
| 8 | Czy podać też kawałek ciasta? *(Shall I also bring a piece of cake?)* | **Tak, poproszę kawałek ciasta.** *(Say yes to a piece of cake.)* | After „kawałek” comes the genitive: ciasto → ciasta. |
| 9 | Oto kawa i bułka. Smacznego! *(Here are your coffee and roll. Enjoy!)* | **Dziękuję bardzo!** *(Thank the waiter.)* | „Dziękuję bardzo” means “thank you very much”. |
| 10 | Czy coś jeszcze? *(Anything else?)* | **Nie, dziękuję. To wszystko.** *(Decline: that is everything.)* | „To wszystko” means “that is all”. |
| 11 | Proszę bardzo! *(You are welcome!)* | — |  |

*Cel:* Get a table for two, order lunch for both of you and do not overpay.  
*Zakończenia:* win: Kasia is delighted: „Teraz rozumiem, dlaczego Marek tyle o panu mówi!” She invites you to a gathering of Marek's friends in the evening. · partial: Lunch was a success, but Kasia had to explain several times to the waiter what you wanted. ## Train to Gdańsk (zug)

### Poziom 3: On the train  (typed=0.5, hint=bank, cierpliwość 4)

Nowe słowa: lubię = I like (lubić), okna = of the window (genitive of okno), zapłacić = to pay, wolne = free, vacant, opóźnienia = of delay (genitive of opóźnienie)

| # | Rozmówca | Uczeń (poprawnie) | Wyjaśnienie |
|---|---|---|---|

*Scena:* The train has started. The conductor walks through the carriage checking tickets, and Marek sits a few rows away, waving at you.  
*Cel:* Show your ticket, choose a seat and get to Gdańsk without trouble.  
*Zakończenia:* win: The conductor returns your ticket with a smile: „Wszystko w porządku!” Marek makes room for you by the window and asks about your first impressions of Kraków. · partial: The conductor finally validated your ticket, but had to repeat himself several times. Marek helps you sit down. · fail: The conductor decided that you do not understand what is said to you. Only Marek checked your ticket, and you get a reprimand.

| 1 | Dzień dobry! Pański bilet, proszę. *(Hello! Your ticket, please.)* | **Oto mój bilet.** *(Show your ticket.)* | „Oto” means “here is”; „mój” is “my” for a masculine noun. |
| 2 | Dziękuję. Czy chce pan siedzieć przy oknie? *(Thank you. Would you like to sit by the window?)* | **Tak, przy oknie, poproszę.** (dobra); złe: Nie, dziękuję, nie lubię okna.; Tak, chcę zapłacić. | The conductor is asking about a seat, not about paying.; „Przy oknie” means “by the window”, not “I do not like the window”. |
| 3 | To miejsce jest wolne. Pociąg ma dziesięć minut opóźnienia. *(This seat is free. The train is ten minutes late.)* | **To nie jest problem. Dziękuję!** *(Say it is not a problem and thank him.)* | „Nie jest problem” means “it is not a problem”. |
| 4 | Szczęśliwej podróży do Gdańska! *(Have a good trip to Gdańsk!)* | — |  |
